THE GEOMETRY CHAMPION!

Made byTeacher Veli

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Students join on their phones with the code from the teacher. They are split automatically into 4 teams (max. 10 students per team).
  2. The teacher shows a question and presses Start Timer. The question appears on every student's phone.
  3. Every student picks an answer alone. A correct answer earns personal points (faster = bigger bonus).
  4. When time is up (or everyone has answered), each team is judged: if at least half of its members are right, the team is Correct: the runner moves ahead, +100 points, +10 coins.
  5. If fewer than half are right, the team is Wrong and loses 1 of its 3 lives. A team with no lives is out of the race, but its students keep earning personal points.
  6. The teacher can still change a team's result with the Correct / Incorrect buttons in the teacher panel.
  7. After question 10, the team with the most points is the Geometry Champion. Ties are broken by students' correct answers, then coins.
